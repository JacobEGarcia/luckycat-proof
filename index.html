THE OBJECT

Nobody loves their speaker. Every voice assistant ships in the same gray puck. Lucky Cat starts from the object: a maneki-neko - four hundred years of beckoning luck into shops and homes - with a real assistant inside.

It hears you across the room, answers from a brain running entirely on the device, waves its paw when it greets you, and glows at the collar when it listens. No account. No cloud round-trip for the core experience. No subscription. Cut the internet - it still works.

The second body proves the platform: the same Genie OS lives in a brass lamp you rub to wake. The cat sells luck; the lamp grants wishes. Lucky Cat Robotics is not a cat company - it is an assistant that wears objects with meaning.
